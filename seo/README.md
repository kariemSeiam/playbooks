# SEO — declarative field notes, spanning two surfaces on purpose

Same shape every declarative field here uses (`../software-engineering/README.md`
documents it canonically): atomic notes, one topic each, 7-part shape.
Its own top-level folder rather than a subfolder of `marketing/` for a
specific reason — it isn't purely client-facing marketing knowledge the
way brand equity or growth loops are. This field covers both what search
and generative engines reward in general (client-applicable, genuinely
declarative) and how that knowledge applies to this operator's own
published work (`github/discoverability-checklist.md` is the procedural
counterpart, living in `github/` where the other publishing checklists
already do). A folder that only fit one side of that split would
misrepresent what's actually in it.

## Notes

- **[fundamentals.md](fundamentals.md)** — the three classic pillars: technical (can
  it be crawled), on-page (is it relevant and well-structured), off-page
  (does anything vouch for it) — and why clients chronically over-invest
  in one while a different pillar is the actual bottleneck.
- **[generative-engine-optimization.md](generative-engine-optimization.md)** — GEO/AEO, the newer discipline
  of being cited inside an LLM's synthesized answer instead of ranked as
  a clicked link. Builds on the three pillars rather than replacing them.
- **[developer-platform-discoverability.md](developer-platform-discoverability.md)** — the three-pillar framework
  adapted to GitHub, npm, and package registries specifically, where
  stars function as the off-page signal and a repo's "About" section is
  the on-page one.
